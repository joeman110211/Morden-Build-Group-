Ask us directly ↗
Do you provide free quotes?

Yes. Use the form or call us with a description of the job. We will discuss what is needed and arrange the next step. A quote does not commit you to proceeding.

What areas do you cover?

We are aiming this site at customers across Ashford, Staines, Sunbury, Feltham, Shepperton and nearby Surrey and Middlesex areas. Confirm the exact service area before publishing.

Can you help with both small repairs and larger projects?

Yes, depending on the work and availability. Tell us what you need and we can confirm whether it is a service we can take on.

Can I send photos to help explain the job?

Yes. For now, use the contact details to send photos after making contact. A secure photo-upload option can be added if needed.

Are you insured and licensed?

Please ask us to confirm the relevant insurance and any licences required for your particular job. We will only publish specific claims once the details have been verified.
